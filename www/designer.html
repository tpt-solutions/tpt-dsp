Gain is normalised to unity at DC, the convention of this crate. For an even-order Chebyshev I filter, DC is therefore the ripple peak (0 dB) rather than the ripple trough (−ripple dB) that scipy reports. Band-pass and band-stop edges for Chebyshev II and elliptic designs are the points where the response reaches the stopband attenuation. Coefficients use y = b0·x + b1·x₋₁ + b2·x₋₂ − a1·y₋₁ − a2·y₋₂ with a0 = 1.
